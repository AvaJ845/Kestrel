Privacy Policy

Kestrel Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Kestrel collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, and no tracking. Everything the app does runs on your device.

What Kestrel does with data

  • No data collected. Kestrel has no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • No account. You never create a login or provide any personal information.
  • On-device only. Your list of watched weather stations and display preferences are stored locally on your device and never leave it.

Network requests

To score atmospheric anomalies, Kestrel fetches public weather data for the stations you choose, directly from your device:

  • Open-Meteo — current temperature, recent hourly readings, and daily-high forecasts.
  • Aviation Weather Center (METAR) — an independent sensor reading used to cross-validate.

These requests contain only the coordinates or station identifier of a station you are watching. They include no personal identifiers. Kestrel does not send your data to us or to any third party, because it holds no personal data to send.

Children

Kestrel is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
